Delete Your Lume Scan Account

Lume Scan by DarkWave Studios LLC

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

  1. Open Lume Scan and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account, then confirm.

If the app asks you to sign in again, sign out, sign back in, and repeat the steps (this is a security check).

Option 2 — By email

Email support@lumescan.tech from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my account”. We will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email address and name)
  • Your entitlement / license records (Pro status)
  • Settings saved on your phone for that account

What is kept

  • Vehicle diagnostic data is never stored on our servers, so there is nothing to delete there.
  • Payment records held by our payment processor (Stripe) may be retained as required by tax and financial law.
  • Condition Report hashes anchored to the Trust Layer Ledger are one-way fingerprints that contain no personal information and cannot be removed.
